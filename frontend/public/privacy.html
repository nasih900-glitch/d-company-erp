D Company ERP Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 July 2026

D Company ERP is a private operations app for authorized D Company staff, owners, and approved business users. It is used to run cafe POS, orders, inventory, sessions, reports, pricing, settings, and audit workflows. Sign-in is required.

Short version:

D Company ERP does not sell personal data, does not show third-party ads, and does not track users across other companies' apps or websites. It processes the operational data needed to run and protect the business.

Information Processed

Depending on your role and how the app is used, D Company ERP may process account details such as name, email address, user ID, role, login status, and audit activity.

Business records may include orders, invoices, payments, refunds, customer contact details entered for billing or service, GST or tax details when entered, menu and pricing changes, inventory, suppliers, stock movement, staff actions, reports, P&L summaries, gaming sessions, shisha sessions, streaming sessions, and audit logs.

If an authorized user chooses OCR or document scanning, the app may process receipt, bill, or business-document images for that workflow.

Technical Logs

The app and backend may process technical information such as device identifiers, IP address, requested URL, timestamps, authentication events, error logs, and security logs for reliability, access control, fraud prevention, and abuse prevention.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate users, run POS and billing, manage inventory and sessions, generate reports, maintain audit records, protect the system, troubleshoot problems, and meet accounting, tax, legal, and compliance obligations.

Tracking and Advertising

D Company ERP does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not track users across other companies' apps or websites for advertising.

Sharing

D Company does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with service providers needed for hosting, security, email delivery, payments or hardware integrations if enabled, professional advisers, accountants, or public authorities where required by law.

Retention

Business, tax, finance, and audit records are retained for as long as required for business operations, legal obligations, tax compliance, dispute resolution, and system security.

Security and Access

Access is role-based and limited to authorized users. Sensitive actions may be recorded in audit logs. Users should keep passwords private and report suspicious access immediately.

Children

D Company ERP is a business operations app and is not intended for children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact D Company through the support channel listed on the support page.
