D Company ERP Support

Support for authorized D Company ERP users.

Need help?

Contact the D Company owner or system administrator for sign-in issues, POS problems, inventory questions, reports, audit access, pricing/settings changes, or live operations support.

What This App Provides

  • Private POS, order, billing, and receipt workflows.
  • Inventory, stock movement, supplier, and pricing controls.
  • Gaming, VR, simulator, shisha, and streaming session management.
  • Reports, analytics, P&L, staff workflows, and audit logs.

Access

This is a private ERP app. It requires an authorized D Company account. If you cannot sign in, ask the owner or administrator to confirm your account, role, and device access.

Operations Support

For urgent live-shop issues, use the internal support route set by D Company. Do not share passwords, owner access, customer records, payment information, or audit logs through public channels.
